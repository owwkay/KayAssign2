Handheld Windmill DIY Craft

Materials:
  • Sheets of foam
  • Scissors
  • Glue dots OR Sticky tape OR PVA glue
  • Ruler
  • Pen
  • Paper straws
  • Pipecleaners
  • Hole puncher
Steps:
  1. Using a ruler and a pen, measure a 12 and a half centimeter square and cut it out.
  2. Fold the foam diagonally in half, and then again for the other direction.
  3. Make a hole in the middle where the creases meet with a pen or hole punch. Then cut from each corner crease, halfway to the middle to form flaps.
  4. For each flap, poke a hole in the corner until there are 4 in total (not including the middle). Place a little bit of glue under each hole.
  5. Bring each point to the middle so that the holes are aligned.
  6. Take an extra piece of foam and use a gluestick lid to make two circular impressions, then cut them out and poke holes in the middle.
  7. Take the paper straw and wrap a bit of the end of a pipecleaner to it.
  8. Thread the first circle into the pipecleaner, and then thread the pipecleaner through the windmill, from the back to the front.
  9. Thread the second circle through the pipecleaner, and make sure there is a small gap for the windmill to be able to move.
  10. Cut off the excess pipecleaner and bend the end into itself so that everything is secured.
  11. Spin your windmill and have fun!
