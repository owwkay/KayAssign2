Ingredients:

  • Bread
  • Jam
  • Sliced ​​cheese
  • Sliced ​​ham
  • Egg
  • Bread crumbs
  • Cooking oil
  • Butter
Recipe: 1. Cut the crust by overlapping three pieces of bread. 2. Spread strawberry jam on a slice of bread. Then cheese, ham, and cheese are placed in that order. Next spread strawberry jam on another slice and place on the sandwich, jam side down. Repeat once. 3. Fill the pan with cooking oil, and then fry the bread in the order of egg water, bread crumbs, and rotate it and fry it on all sides.
